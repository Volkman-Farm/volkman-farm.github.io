Most of what we've written about cooking with microgreens is about keeping them whole. Add them last, keep them off the heat, let the shape show.

A blender throws all of that out. Once the blade has been through, the leaf doesn't matter anymore. Flavor and color are what's left, and those are the 2 things a small green carries in a big way.

Our clamshells say wash before using, so wash what you're about to blend and shake it dry. Extra water thins a sauce.

## Pesto

Basil pesto with a handful of [kale](/greens/kale/) blended in comes out a deeper green and goes further. Kale's mild, so the basil still leads. Start with about a quarter of the greens as kale and go up from there.

For a pesto with an edge, use [arugula](/greens/arugula/) for some of the basil. It brings pepper instead of sweetness, and it's very good on a steak or stirred into warm white beans.

Same as always with pesto: nuts, garlic, a hard cheese, olive oil, salt, lemon. Pulse, don't run it, and stop while it still has some texture.

## Green sauce

[Cilantro](/greens/cilantro/) is the one for this. Blend it with a jalapeño, garlic, lime juice, a little oil, and either a spoonful of yogurt or half an avocado to hold it together. Put it on tacos, eggs, rice, or grilled chicken.

A tray of cilantro micros is mostly stem and young leaf, so the whole thing goes in. No picking leaves off.

## A dressing that turns the salad green

Olive oil, vinegar, a spoon of mustard, a little honey, and a big pinch of [broccoli](/greens/broccoli/). Blend until smooth. Broccoli's mild enough that the dressing tastes like dressing, just bright green.

## Make extra

Blended sauces are the one form of microgreens that freezes well, because the blade already did what freezing would have done. We covered that in [the freezing post](/blog/can-i-freeze-microgreens/): ice cube tray, then a bag.

Next time you're at the end of a clamshell with a handful left and no plan, put it in the blender with whatever's in the herb drawer. Taste it, salt it, and write down what you did if it's good.
