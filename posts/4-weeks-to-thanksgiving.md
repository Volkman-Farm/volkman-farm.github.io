Thanksgiving is 4 weeks from today.

For most of what we grow, that's plenty of runway. For cilantro it isn't.

On Oct 1 we counted back from the table and said a Thanksgiving tray of [cilantro](/greens/cilantro/) goes in around Nov 5. Two things have pulled that date earlier since.

The first we flagged the next day: [October clocks run long](/blog/the-shed-in-october/). The 18 to 21 days on the cilantro page is a summer number, and every tray in the shed is taking longer than that now. We don't have an October number to give you. Only that it's longer.

The second we should've caught on Oct 1. A tray doesn't have to be ready by Thursday dinner. It has to be ready by the delivery day before it, and for most of the service area that's earlier than Wednesday.

## Counting from the delivery day

Thanksgiving week keeps the usual days. Using 21 days, the long end of the summer range:

- **Seminole County, Friday Nov 20.** The tray goes in Oct 30. Tomorrow.
- **Downtown, Sunday Nov 22.** Nov 1.
- **North Orlando, Monday Nov 23 or Wednesday Nov 25.** Nov 2 or Nov 4.

October runs past the long end, so read every one of those as "no later than."

The faster greens have room. [Kale](/greens/kale/) at 8 to 12 days and pea tendrils at 10 to 14 go in around the second week of November for any of those days. Give them a few extra days for the season and there's still slack.

If cilantro's going on your Thanksgiving table, [put the order in](/order/) this week and tell us the date you're cooking. We'll count back from your delivery day.
