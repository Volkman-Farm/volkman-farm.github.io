In September we said [radish, kohlrabi, and Swiss chard were out](/blog/which-green-fits-your-kitchen/), and that we'd say so when they were back.

They aren't coming back on their own. From here on we grow those 3 when somebody asks for one, and not otherwise.

7 stay on the rack: [arugula](/greens/arugula/), [beet](/greens/beet/), [broccoli](/greens/broccoli/), [cilantro](/greens/cilantro/), [kale](/greens/kale/), [mustard](/greens/mustard/), and [pea tendrils](/greens/pea/). Those are the ones we offer every week.

The other 3 still have their pages, so you can see what you'd be asking for:

- **[Radish](/greens/radish/).** Peppery, crunchy, daikon type. 6 to 8 days.
- **[Kohlrabi](/greens/kohlrabi/).** Mild, crisp stem, a little broccoli-sweet. 9 to 11 days.
- **[Swiss chard](/greens/chard/).** Rainbow stems, flavor between spinach and beet. 10 to 14 days.

Those are summer clocks. Everything in the shed [runs longer in October](/blog/the-shed-in-october/), so give us more notice for these than you would for the 7. Same price as everything else, $3.50 an ounce.

If you want one, pick it on the [order form](/order/) and tell us the date you'd want it on the table. We'll count back from there.
