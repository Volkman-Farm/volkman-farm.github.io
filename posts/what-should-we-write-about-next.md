Every post on this blog since July 7 came off one calendar. We wrote it in July, 13 weeks of topics in 7 lanes: the greens, the kitchen, the homestead, the yard, the route, the shed, and questions.

It runs out today.

Before we write the next one, we'd like to hear what you want in it. A few places to start:

- Something about microgreens you've wondered and never looked up.
- A post of ours you wanted more of. Or less of.
- A green you've seen somewhere and want to know if we'd grow it.
- Something on the homestead you want followed. The chickens, the [front field](/blog/cover-crop-check-in/), the trees we're [planting next year](/blog/what-were-designing-for-next-year/).
- A meal you'd like us to work greens into.

Small questions count. Some of our favorite posts answered one sentence, like [how long do they last](/blog/how-long-do-microgreens-last/) or [can I freeze them](/blog/can-i-freeze-microgreens/).

Email us at [micros@volkman.farm](mailto:micros@volkman.farm). One line is plenty. We read all of it, and anything that turns into a post gets answered here.

Send us the question you'd type into a search bar at 10 pm.
