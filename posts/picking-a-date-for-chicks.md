The brooder's been empty since Oct 4, when the last 2 birds in it [went back to the flock](/blog/back-with-the-flock/). New chicks are next. Then a second chicken tractor.

What we're doing right now is staring at a calendar.

Chicks in a brooder need somebody home. Every day, for weeks. So the date we pick has to clear the holidays coming up, and some travel we've got on the books further out. Pick wrong and the first weeks of a chick's life land on a week nobody's here.

So we're working it backward. Find the stretch where we're home, count back, order for the front of it.

We haven't landed on it yet. When we do, it'll go here.

It's funny which part ends up slow. A month ago the chicks were waiting on 2 hurt birds. Now the birds are fine and the chicks are waiting on us.

If you keep birds and travel, tell us how you handle it at [micros@volkman.farm](mailto:micros@volkman.farm). A neighbor who checks in? Staying home until they're feathered out? We'd like to know what works.
