A wok runs hotter than anything else on the stove, and a microgreen is mostly water in a very thin wall. Put them in together at the start and you get a dark green smear on the bottom of the pan.

So they go in last. After the sauce, after the burner's off.

Wash the portion you're using first, the way the clamshell label says, and get it properly dry. Water hitting hot oil spits, and wet greens steam into a clump instead of tossing loose.

## Pea tendrils

[Pea tendrils](/greens/pea/) are the one green we grow that can take a little heat. Pea shoots are a stir-fry vegetable in their own right in a lot of Chinese cooking, usually with nothing more than garlic and oil.

Ours are younger and thinner than what you'd buy by the bunch, so give them 20 or 30 seconds at most. Kill the burner, throw in a big handful, toss twice, and plate it. They'll go glossy and soft at the bottom and stay crisp on top.

## Fried rice

Fried rice wants something sharp at the end to cut through the oil and soy. Two good choices:

- **[Cilantro](/greens/cilantro/).** Stir a handful through right before it leaves the pan, then put more on top raw. A lot of fried rice gets cilantro anyway; micro cilantro saves you the chopping.
- **[Mustard](/greens/mustard/).** A small pinch on each bowl. It brings heat without a chili, and the heat builds, so go lighter than you think.

## Everything else on top

[Broccoli](/greens/broccoli/) and [kale](/greens/kale/) don't belong in the pan. They belong on the bowl, raw, after it's served. Both are mild enough to sit on a beef and broccoli or a garlic chicken stir-fry without changing what it tastes like, and they add a cold crunch against hot food.

[Arugula](/greens/arugula/) goes on top too, and it's especially good on anything with a sweet sauce, teriyaki or orange chicken, where the pepper keeps it from tasting like dessert.

About a loose handful per plate is plenty.

Next time you make fried rice, split it. Half the pan with greens stirred in at the end, half with them only on top. Eat both and decide which one your house wants.
