A basket of rolls can get through dinner just fine with a dish of plain butter. It’s still nice to have a second little dish with something in it. If everybody’s already using the kitchen, this is one thing you can make at the end of the counter with a fork.

Try a spoonful of softened salted butter and a small pinch of [radish microgreens](https://volkman.farm/greens/radish/). Chop the greens finer than you would for a salad. You want a few green flecks in each swipe of butter, without getting a whole stem when you pull the roll away. Mash them together with the fork and taste a little on the torn edge of a roll. If you’d like it sharper, add another pinch.

Leave the rest of the butter plain. Someone can have a peppery bite, someone else can have the roll they were looking forward to, and there’s nothing to explain when the basket comes around the table again.

A spoonful also makes sense when the big dinner is over and there’s one last roll in the basket. You don’t have to wait for another holiday to make it.
