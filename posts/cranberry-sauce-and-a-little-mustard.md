Cranberry sauce can be sweet enough that a bite of turkey and a spoonful of sauce taste mostly like sauce. A few [mustard microgreens](/greens/mustard/) bring back a little of the savory bite. They're sharp enough that you don't need more than a few.

Try it on your own plate first. Put a spoonful of cranberry sauce beside the turkey, then lay two or three shoots across the top. Take a bite with both. If your sauce is already quite tart, you might like it better with fewer shoots. A sweeter sauce can take a little more.

There's no need to stir the greens through the serving bowl. The sharp part of the bite is easier to taste when the shoots are still on top. And if someone wants their cranberry sauce exactly the way it always tastes, they can have it that way.

The same pairing works with a piece of sharp cheddar. A dab of sauce and a couple of shoots are enough. It's a good way to find out what mustard greens taste like if you've only seen them scattered over a dish at a restaurant.
