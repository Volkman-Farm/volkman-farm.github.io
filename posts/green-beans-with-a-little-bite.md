When the green beans are already buttered and the rest of dinner is nearly on the table, there's still time to taste one. If it tastes flat, a squeeze of lemon might do more good than another pinch of salt. Squeeze a wedge over a serving first. The next bean will tell you whether to do the rest.

For a little bite, snip a few [radish microgreens](/greens/radish/) over the lemony beans. The stems have some crunch, and the leaves bring a peppery heat you can still pick out against the butter. Use kitchen scissors to cut the longer stems once or twice so they'll come up with the beans on a fork.

If you're adding toasted almonds, put them on after the lemon so they keep their crunch. Go easy on the radish the first time, especially if there’s already a lot of black pepper in the dish. You can always pinch a few more over your plate.
