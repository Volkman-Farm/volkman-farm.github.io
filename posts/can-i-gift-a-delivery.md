Yes. It's one of the better gifts you can give somebody who cooks, and it works a little differently than ordering for yourself.

You tell us who they are and how to reach them. We text them directly to set up the drop and to find out what they want, because guessing somebody else's greens is how a gift becomes a chore. Then it lands on their porch like any other delivery.

You pay us. They don't, and they never see a bill. We settle up with you directly, so nothing awkward happens at their door.

The one requirement is the address. They have to be somewhere we already drive, which means one of the [neighborhoods on the route](/blog/do-you-deliver-to-my-street/). We can't add a stop across the metro for a single delivery without breaking the route for everybody else on it, and a route that runs long is a route where the last porch gets worse greens than the first.

A couple of practical notes. Tell us if it's a surprise, because our text is going to give it away otherwise and we'd rather know that going in. And tell us whether it's one delivery or a run of them, since a month of Fridays is a very different gift from a single clamshell and both are fine.

The people this lands best with are the ones who cook a lot and buy for themselves carefully. They'll notice the difference in a way that somebody who eats out most nights won't.

If somebody came to mind while you were reading this, [start the order](/order/) and put their name in it instead of yours. We'll take it from there.
