There's no cart on this site. No checkout, no card field, no account to make.

The [order page](/order/) asks 4 things. Where you are, which green you want to start with, how many ounces, and a phone number and email. There's a box to tick if you'd like it as a weekly standing order (that's the [10% off](/blog/subscribe-and-save-how-the-10-percent-works/), skip any week), and one to say it's okay for us to contact you.

Then you hit send.

## Where it goes

It goes to me. The form sends an email to my inbox.

From there I get in touch with you myself to confirm your first delivery day and the exact total. Nothing's charged when you hit send.

Everything comes out of [one small shed](/blog/the-shed-albert-built/), and a tray of cilantro is 18 to 21 days from seed. So what you've sent is closer to a question than a purchase: can we have this for you, and when?

## Picking the day

Your delivery day comes out of the 3 routes, and which one depends on where you are.

- **Friday morning:** Seminole County. Sanford, Lake Mary, Winter Springs, Oviedo, Casselberry.
- **Monday and Wednesday evenings:** north Orlando.
- **Sunday afternoon:** downtown.

The full list of neighborhoods is on the [delivery page](/delivery/). If your street's close to one of them but not on it, send the form anyway and say so in the conversation that follows. The routes were drawn around [a family calendar](/blog/why-we-stay-small-on-purpose/), and calendars change.

## What it isn't

It isn't instant. You won't get a confirmation number or a tracking link. You'll get a person.

Before you fill it in, decide which green you'd start with if you could only have one. It's the second question on the form.
