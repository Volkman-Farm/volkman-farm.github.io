A baked potato with butter and sour cream is a perfectly good dinner. It doesn't need to become a grain bowl or lose the sour cream before there's room for something fresh.

[Mustard microgreens](/greens/mustard/) would be our pick if you like a sharp bite. The potato is mild, the butter is rich, and a few mustard shoots give you something different in the same forkful. You can still taste the potato.

Cheddar works here too. So do the chives you were already going to use. We're not suggesting you replace your favorite toppings with a pile of greens.

The amount matters more than making the potato look finished. A thick heap of mustard can take over. A few shoots leave room for the other flavors, especially if there's already pepper or hot sauce on the plate. [Arugula](/greens/arugula/) is another peppery option, with a different flavor worth trying if you already enjoy it in salads.

There's also the potato skin. If you've baked it crisp and like eating it, you already have a good contrast to the soft middle. Microgreens aren't needed to rescue that texture. They're another ingredient you might enjoy, not a correction to the meal.

Wash the greens you're using under running water and dry them with a clean towel before adding them. The [FDA recommends running water, not soap or produce wash](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/selecting-and-serving-produce-safely). They go on your plate after the potato has finished baking, not into the oven with it.

No second pan, no dressing to mix. Butter, sour cream, a few greens, and the potato you wanted in the first place.
