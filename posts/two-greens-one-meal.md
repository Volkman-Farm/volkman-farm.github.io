Cut a piece of toast in half. Put the same spread on both sides, then put a different microgreen on each.

You don't need a special meal to find out which greens you like. You need one you'll eat again.

Pick 2 varieties that sound different to you. [Pea tendrils](/greens/pea/) are sweet and crisp. [Arugula](/greens/arugula/) brings pepper. You could also try mild [broccoli](/greens/broccoli/) beside [mustard](/greens/mustard/), which has a sharper, horseradish-like bite. Use whatever 2 greens you have or choose to order. Our [kitchen guide](/blog/which-green-fits-your-kitchen/) has more ideas if you're deciding where to start.

Our clamshells say wash before using. Take out the portions you'll eat, wash them, and dry them. Leave the rest in the fridge.

Keep the toast, spread, and amount of greens close to the same on both halves. Start with a small pinch of each. Mustard can bring more heat, so add a little at a time if that's new to you.

Take a bite from each side. Notice the stem as well as the leaf. Does one have more crunch? Does the flavor stay in the background or come through the spread? Take another bite if you're not sure. You might like one on its own and the other better with the rest of the meal.

Toast isn't required. Split a sandwich, or put the same eggs in 2 small bowls and add the greens after cooking. Keep the other ingredients the same so you can taste what changes.

Write down which side you'd make again and what you ate it with. Next time, use the green you picked on another meal you already make. If you want to try another variety after that, our guide to [plant families and swaps](/blog/four-families-on-one-shelf/) gives you a place to look.
