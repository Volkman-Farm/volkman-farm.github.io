There are 7 greens on the rack and about 7 things on a Thanksgiving table that want one. They line up better than you'd expect.

**Deviled eggs: [mustard](/greens/mustard/).** Horseradish heat on a rich yolk. A few stems per egg, no more. It builds.

**Goat cheese crostini: [beet](/greens/beet/).** Goat cheese, a drizzle of honey, a small pile on top. The pink stems show against the white, and the earthy flavor sits well with honey.

**Squash soup: [broccoli](/greens/broccoli/).** Mild enough that it won't fight the soup. Scatter it on the bowls right before they go out, not in the pot.

**Sweet potatoes: [arugula](/greens/arugula/).** Sweet wants pepper. We covered this one in [the roast vegetable post](/blog/roast-vegetables-finished-cold/), and it holds for a casserole dish too.

**Roasted carrots: [cilantro](/greens/cilantro/).** Carrots with cumin and a little lime, cilantro on top. If your family includes a cilantro hater, this is the dish they can skip without missing the turkey.

**Stuffing or green beans: [kale](/greens/kale/).** Kale micros are soft and sweet, with none of the chew of grown kale. A handful on top of something brown and savory gives it a fresh edge.

**The side salad: [pea tendrils](/greens/pea/).** Pea tendrils can be the salad. Lemon vinaigrette, maybe some shaved parmesan, nothing else needed. They're also the sturdiest thing we grow, so they'll hold on a table that sits out through seconds.

You don't need all 7. Pick the 2 or 3 dishes your table can't do without and match those.

Then check the clock. Cilantro is the slow one, and it's [already close](/blog/4-weeks-to-thanksgiving/) for a Thanksgiving tray. The other 6 have more room.

Write your menu down the left side of a page this week. Next to each dish, write the green. Cross out anything that's only there because the list had a slot for it.
