Nothing happened in the run this week, which is the news.

The 2 birds we [moved back in with the flock](/blog/back-with-the-flock/) on Oct 4 have settled in without any trouble.

After the summer we've had out there, a week where nothing goes wrong is worth writing down.

The chick plan is still being worked out. Nothing's ordered. When it is, we'll say so here, along with the second chicken tractor that comes after it.

Out front, the cover crop's where we left it, mowed and waiting. The food forest hasn't changed either.

The front field question from last week is still open, too: what goes on three-quarters of an acre of poorly drained sand coming off a summer cover crop. If you've got an answer, [micros@volkman.farm](mailto:micros@volkman.farm) is where it goes.

Go look at something on your own place that's been quiet for a week. Write down that it was. In a year you'll want to know which weeks those were.
