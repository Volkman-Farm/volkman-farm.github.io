By the time the stuffing comes out of the oven, most of the table is headed in the same direction. Warm, soft, a little buttery. A bowl of sliced apple and cabbage gives you something cold and crisp to eat between bites. It can sit right next to the stuffing without asking anybody to give up the stuffing.

A tart apple works well here. Cut it thin, add a little shredded cabbage and toss them with lemon and a pinch of salt. A few [radish microgreens](/greens/radish/) give the apple a peppery edge. If you want something milder, [pea tendrils](/greens/pea/) keep more of the apple’s sweetness in the bite. Either one works in a small amount. You still want to get a forkful of apple and cabbage.

We like this kind of side because it doesn’t need an oven rack. The bowl can go in the fridge while everything else is cooking, and the greens can go on just before it comes to the table. There’s no need to fit one more hot dish between the rolls and the turkey.

If you’ve got an apple and a few greens now, try the two together before you make a bowl for everybody. The rest of the apple is still yours for lunch.
